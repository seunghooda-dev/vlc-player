MXF QC Player V.1.0 - Broadcast Sample Checklist
================================================

Use this checklist before copying a release to another PC or after changing
VLC/FFmpeg/player logic.

Required Smoke Samples
----------------------
[ ] OP1a MXF, 1920x1080, 29.97 DF
[ ] XDCAM HD MXF
[ ] MXF with 2 audio channels
[ ] MXF with 4 or 8 audio channels
[ ] MXF with known black section
[ ] MXF with known mute section
[ ] 20-minute or longer MXF for stability playback

Minimum Checks
--------------
[ ] CUE shows the first frame without black video
[ ] Play starts video and audio together
[ ] Default audio output is channel 1/2
[ ] Channel checkbox changes audio without blanking video
[ ] Audio meters show up to 8 channels
[ ] Loudness S/I display updates during playback
[ ] Black detection finds a known black section
[ ] Mute detection finds a known mute section
[ ] Stop closes child ffmpeg/ffplay processes
[ ] App exit leaves no packaged ffmpeg/ffplay process behind

Scripts
-------
Startup and dependency check:

preflight_check.bat

Single MXF smoke test:

smoke_mxf_test.bat "C:\path\sample.mxf"

Layout check:

ui_layout_check.bat

Desktop or folder sample validation:

broadcast_sample_validation.bat "C:\path\sample-folder"

Fixed sample-set validation:

broadcast_sample_set_validation.bat BROADCAST_SAMPLE_SET.txt

The BAT wrapper calls broadcast_sample_set_validation.ps1 so every row in the
set file is processed and summarized in one report.

Long playback check:

stability_mxf_test.bat "C:\path\long_sample.mxf" 1800 30

Notes
-----
The scripts do not delete source MXF files. Reports are written under:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\MXF QC Player V.1.0\reports
