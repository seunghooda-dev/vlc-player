Third-Party Notices
===================

MXF QC Player can use external media tools that are not authored by this
project.

VLC / libVLC
------------
Website: https://www.videolan.org/vlc/

VLC is required for MXF video playback. If VLC is installed separately on the
target PC, this package does not redistribute VLC.

FFmpeg
------
Website: https://ffmpeg.org/

FFmpeg, FFprobe, and FFplay may be copied into the portable package tools\
folder by package_release.bat when they are available on the build PC.

FFmpeg builds can be licensed under LGPL or GPL depending on build options.
When distributing a package that includes FFmpeg binaries, keep the relevant
license files and source-code offer from the FFmpeg build provider together
with the released package.
